
This protocol is to be used when a head injury is suspected.

Start [[Dexamethasone]] as soon as possible to help with cranial/brain inflammation. Continue until improvement is witnessed (my experience is 2-4 days) 

Provide electrostat 1-2 hours after initial receipt.  

Keep in basket on heat for at least 48 hrs.

Provide quite and dark environment. 

Monitor for brain injury symptoms such as spinning in circles, blindness, balance and coordination issues.  

Although the physical aspects of head injuries can heal within a few days, the brain may take months and possible years to fully heal.
