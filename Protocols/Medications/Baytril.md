## Use

Injectable or oral antibiotic — **aerobic gram-negative and many gram-positive**
bacteria. **Not effective against anaerobes or Streptococci.** Best choice for
**animal bites** (distributes well through the body, effective against
*Chlamydia*). May work better combined with [[Clavamox]] for trauma. Used for
dermal infections, wounds/abscesses, intestinal, respiratory, and some urinary
infections.

- **Give with [[BeneBac]]** while on course.

## Dosing

- **5 mg/kg (low) or 10 mg/kg (high)**, PO/SQ/IM, **BID — or doubled and given
  once a day** (operator's standard practice, confirmed by this chart).
- Course: **5-7+ days**.
- **Under 200g: use the 1:10 dilution.** **Over 200g: use full strength.**
  (Practical tip from the chart: for squirrels under 200g, pull 0.1cc of water
  first, then draw the small Baytril volume into it — same technique as the
  operator's own Clarice dilution.)
- Very bad taste undiluted.
- Not for extended use in rapidly-growing animals — cartilage abnormality risk.
- Protect from direct sunlight; check bottle expiration.
- Evaluation period: 5-7 days.

## Cautions

- Cartilage effects in juveniles are described in some species — use the
  lowest effective dose in young, fast-growing animals and keep the course
  short.
- Can sting on SQ injection; dilution helps.
- Ensure good hydration.
- Not effective against anaerobes/Streptococci — if aspiration/anaerobic
  involvement is suspected, pair with [[Clavamox]] rather than relying on
  Baytril alone (matches the actual regimen used for [[Cass]] and [[Abby]]).
