> [!warning] Verify before use
> Doses below are the commonly published wildlife-rehab ranges for tree squirrels.
> Confirm concentration, dose, and interval with your supervising vet before relying on this.

## Use

Broad-spectrum antibiotic. Common in rehab for cat-bite prophylaxis ([[cat-bite]]),
respiratory infection / clicking, and aspiration pneumonia.

## Dosing

- **5–10 mg/kg**, SQ or PO, every 12–24 h.
- Course typically 7–10 days; complete the full course.
- Dilute for accurate small-volume dosing in neonates.

### Dose from weight (at 22.7 mg/mL)

| Weight | 5 mg/kg | 10 mg/kg |
| ------ | ------- | -------- |
| 50 g   | 0.011 mL | 0.022 mL |
| 100 g  | 0.022 mL | 0.044 mL |
| 250 g  | 0.055 mL | 0.110 mL |
| 500 g  | 0.110 mL | 0.220 mL |

## Cautions

- Cartilage effects in juveniles are described in some species — use the lowest
  effective dose in young, fast-growing animals and keep the course short.
- Can sting on SQ injection; dilution helps.
- Ensure good hydration.
