> [!warning] Verify before use
> Confirm appropriateness with your supervising vet, especially for wounds near
> mucous membranes or the eyes.

## Use

Topical antimicrobial for burns, degloving injuries, and open wounds. Keeps the
wound bed moist and lowers bacterial load.

## Application

- Clean the wound, then apply a **thin layer** of 1% cream.
- Reapply every 12–24 h; cover if practical.
- No weight-based dosing — topical only.

## Cautions

- Avoid getting it in eyes or mouth.
- Sulfa drug — note any prior sulfonamide reaction.
- Discontinue once healthy granulation tissue is established.

## History

Log each application in the patient's **## Medications** table (drug =
`Silvadene`, dose = `thin layer`, route = `topical`).
