> [!warning] Verify before use
> From the SWR (May 2026) squirrel dosage chart. Confirm concentration, dose,
> and course length with your supervising vet before relying on this —
> especially before continuing past 3 days.

## Use

Broad-spectrum antibiotic with real activity against **coccidia** (sulfonamides
interfere with *Eimeria* folate synthesis), in addition to many bacterial GI
and respiratory pathogens. Reasonable choice when a squirrel presents with
diarrhea/GI upset and a dedicated coccidiostat ([[Ponazuril]]) isn't available,
or for general antibacterial cover.

## Dosing

- **30 mg/kg**, PO, BID.
- Formulation on this chart: **48 mg/mL** (pediatric suspension,
  200 mg sulfamethoxazole + 40 mg trimethoprim per 5 mL).
- **BID for 3 days, then consult DVM** before continuing — this is not an
  open-ended course.
- Ensure the animal is well hydrated (sulfonamides risk crystalluria in a
  dehydrated patient).

## Cautions

- Confirm hydration before dosing; add fluids if there's any deficit.
- Note any prior sulfonamide reaction.
- Not a substitute for a confirmed coccidia diagnosis/treatment
  ([[Ponazuril]]) if that's accessible — this chart caps it at a 3-day trial
  specifically because it isn't the first-line coccidiostat.
