## Use

**Treatment of (preclinical) coccidiosis.** Works quickly — faster onset than
[[Albon]]'s longer course. First-line choice alongside Albon for suspected
coccidia (e.g. yellow/liquid diarrhea, bloating, poor weight gain despite good
appetite in a juvenile squirrel — directly relevant to [[OB]]'s case).

- **Give with [[BeneBac]]** (or equivalent probiotic) per formulary-wide note
  — not currently on hand either, see that file.

## Dosing

- **7 mg/kg**, PO, SID (once daily) for **3-5 days**.
- Formulation on hand: **25 mg/mL (2.5%)** — use directly, no dilution.
- Evaluate response at **3-5 days**.

## Cautions

- **Refrigerate.**
- Expiration per bottle/container.
- These volumes are small (0.01-0.03 mL for squirrel-sized patients) — draw
  carefully; consider a 1:10 dilution anyway if you find it hard to measure
  precisely, even though it isn't required at this concentration the way it
  would be for a 5% product.
- **If a different toltrazuril product is ever used instead of this one**,
  check its concentration before dosing from this table — a 5% (50 mg/mL)
  product is 2x this strength and would need diluting 1:10 first to use these
  same numbers safely.
