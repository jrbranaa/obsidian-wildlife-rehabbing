## Use

Oral antibiotic that works on bacterial infections, **especially coccidiosis** —
one of the two dedicated coccidiostats in this formulary (the other is
[[Baycox]]). Low dosage, long-lasting, rapidly-absorbed long-acting sulfonamide.
Also used for respiratory tract, genito-urinary, protozoal enteric, and soft
tissue infections.

- Tastes good — one of the easier oral antibiotics to get into a squirrel.
- **Give with [[BeneBac]]** (or equivalent probiotic) per formulary-wide note.

## Dosing

- **15 mg/kg**, PO, BID.
- Formulation: **50 mg/mL** oral suspension (5%).
- Can be used for **up to 21 days** — a notably long course window compared to
  most other antibiotics on this formulary, suited to coccidiosis's longer
  treatment arc.
- Evaluate response at **2-3 days**.

## History

Directly relevant to any suspected-coccidiosis case where a fecal float isn't
accessible (e.g. [[OB]]) — see [[Ponazuril]] and [[Baycox]] for the other
