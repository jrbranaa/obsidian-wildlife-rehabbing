> From the SWR (May 2026) squirrel dosage chart. Confirm concentration, dose,
> and course length with your supervising vet before relying on this.
>
> **Note a possible inconsistency in the source chart:** the prep instruction
> reads "break a 25 mg capsule into 5 mL water," which works out to 5 mg/mL —
> but the chart's printed concentration is 12.5 mg/mL. Confirm which is
> correct (e.g. whether two capsules go into 5 mL, or the capsule is a
> different strength) before dosing from the table below.

## Use

Anticoagulant rodenticide exposure (warfarin-type toxicity) — supports
clotting factor synthesis. Also used as general coagulation support after
significant trauma/blood loss at some facilities.

## Dosing

- **2.5 mg/kg**, PO, once daily, **for 21 days**.
- Give **with food** (fat-soluble vitamin — absorption improves with a meal).
- Prepared by breaking a capsule into water per the prep note above — see the
  warning about confirming the resulting concentration.

### Dose from weight (at 12.5 mg/mL, per SWR chart — confirm concentration first)

| Weight | Dose (2.5 mg/kg) |
| ------ | ----------------- |
| 50 g   | 0.03 mL |
| 100 g  | 0.05 mL |
| 200 g  | 0.10 mL |
| 300 g  | 0.15 mL |
| 500 g  | 0.25 mL |
| 1000 g | 0.50 mL |

## Cautions

- Only useful if rodenticide (anticoagulant) exposure is suspected/confirmed —
  not a general-purpose supplement.
- Full 21-day course matters — anticoagulant rodenticides have long half-lives;
  stopping early risks relapse of the coagulopathy.
