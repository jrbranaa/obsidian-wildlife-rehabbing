## Use

Oral anti-fungal for **Candida** — yeast and yeast-like fungal infections.
Used for oral or gastrointestinal Candida infections, and crop mycosis
(fungal infection with mycotic diarrhea).

## Dosing

- **100,000 IU/kg**, PO, **QID** (4x/day).
- Formulation: **100,000 IU/mL** liquid.

## Cautions

- Store in a tight, light-resistant container at room temperature; shake well
  before using.
- Deteriorates with heat, light, air, or moisture exposure.
