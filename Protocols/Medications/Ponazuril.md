## Use

First-line anticoccidial in many small-mammal/wildlife formularies — directly
relevant when coccidiosis is suspected (e.g. yellow/liquid diarrhea, bloating,
poor weight gain despite good appetite in a juvenile squirrel). See [[TMS]]
for the fallback option when Ponazuril isn't accessible.

## Dosing

**Not established from this source.** Common veterinary references use
ponazuril (15% oral suspension, 150 mg/mL) in the range of roughly
20 mg/kg PO once daily for 1–3 days for coccidia in small mammals — but this
is general background, not a verified number for squirrels or for whatever
concentration you actually have. Confirm before using.

## Cautions

- Don't substitute the general reference dose above for a confirmed protocol —
  get the real number before treating.
- If a fecal float can confirm coccidia first, that's worth doing before
  committing to a specific antiprotozoal course.
