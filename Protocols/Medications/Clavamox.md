## Use

Oral antibiotic — amoxicillin + clavulanate potassium; the clavulanate makes
the amoxicillin active against a **much broader spectrum**, including many
organisms plain amoxicillin can't touch. Used for urinary tract infections,
skin/soft tissue infections, canine periodontal disease. **Useful against cat
bite *Pasteurella*.** Stable with gastric juices. May work well combined with
[[Baytril]] for trauma (Baytril isn't effective against anaerobes/
Streptococci — Clavamox covers that gap; this is the actual combo used for
[[Cass]] and [[Abby]]).

- **Give with [[BeneBac]]** — Clavamox carries real dysbiosis/diarrhea risk on
  its own, independent of the dose question above.
- **Make sure the animal stays hydrated.**

## Dosing

- **Chart figure: 5.5 mg/kg, PO, BID**, for 5-7+ days. Formulation on the
  chart: 25 mg/mL.
- **The operator's actual bottle concentration is unconfirmed** (see warning
  above) — 62.5 mg/mL is an assumption carried forward from general
  knowledge, not a read label. The table below is only as good as that
  assumption.
- Evaluation period: 5 days.
- Powder/pill form: reconstitute with water, refrigerate after. Generic
  Clavamox is good 30 days after reconstitution; name-brand only 10 days.

## Cautions

- **GI/dysbiosis risk** — watch stool closely, especially in a patient with
  any diarrhea history ([[OB]], [[Abby]]). Probiotic support is not optional
  here.
- Confirm hydration before and during the course.
- **Most meds here arrive portioned/distributed without a printed
  concentration** — this isn't unique to Clavamox. Whenever a dosing note on
  this vault states a concentration without saying how it was confirmed,
  treat it as an assumption, not a verified fact, unless the note says
  otherwise.
