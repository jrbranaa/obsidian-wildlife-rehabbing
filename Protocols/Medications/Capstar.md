## Use

Systemic (PO) flea treatment — very safe for young animals, works quickly,
safe across a wide dosage range. Also used off-label topically for maggot
infestations / wound flushing in some rehab settings.

## Dosing

- **20 mg/kg**, PO, systemic flea treatment.
- Formulation: **50 mg/mL** (matches Capstar's oral suspension).
- Per the prior-formulary chart: for very young/small squirrels (~10g), a
  **1/4 pill** is used instead (for older squirrels) rather than a liquid
  micro-dose.
- Not usually the first choice — try kitten flea powder near (not on) the
  squirrel first.

## Cautions

- Systemic use: standard flea-control cautions apply.
- Topical/off-label use: for maggot/wound-flush purposes only, not standard
  labeling — use clinical judgment for wound care context.
