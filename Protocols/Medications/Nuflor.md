## Use

**Oral antibiotic for respiratory distress, especially pneumonia** caused by
chilling or aspiration. Described as "very strong."

## Dosing

- **150 mg/kg**, PO — given as a **double dose, one time only**.
- **Do not repeat within 48-72 hours.** A second dose is not generally needed;
  if given, it should be a single (not double) dose.
- Formulation: **300 mg/mL**.
- Evaluate response at **2-3 days**.

## Cautions

- Very strong — this is a single big dose, not a daily-course drug like most
  others on this formulary. Don't re-dose within the 48-72h window.
