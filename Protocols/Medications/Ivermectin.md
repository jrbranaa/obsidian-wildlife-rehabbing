## Use

Broad-spectrum anti-parasitic for **ectoparasites and other skin-disorder
parasites** — mange, mites, and other skin conditions causing fur loss (see
[[Loss of Fur]]).

## Dosing / administration

- **Not dosed by mL per body weight** on this chart — administered by a fixed
  small placement:
  - **One drop placed behind one ear**, **once a week for 2-3 weeks**,
    depending on progress, **or**
  - **One drop fed on a nut**, or injected into a nut, once a week for 2-3
    weeks.
- Repeat weekly; response with skin conditions may take time to become
  visible.

## Cautions

- **Very strong** — get help from an experienced rehabber if unfamiliar with
  dosing/handling.
- Diluted, it can also be used to wash an area affected by maggots, or via
  skin absorption for topical parasite control.
