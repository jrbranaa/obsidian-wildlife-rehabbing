## Use

Maintains/restores intestinal flora disrupted by antibiotic use. **Give with
every antibiotic on this formulary** — it's flagged as a footnote on nearly
every medication page in the source chart ("Use BeneBac with all antibiotics").

Per the manual: if flora gets out of balance from antibiotic use without
probiotic support, stool may turn **orange, rust-colored, or green** — a
useful sign to recognize as antibiotic-related dysbiosis rather than a new
problem.

## Dosing

- No fixed mg/kg — dosed by matching volume to the antibiotic, per the
  manual's own rule of thumb: **give roughly the same volume of BeneBac as
  the antibiotic dose** (e.g. 0.1 cc Clavamox → 0.1 cc BeneBac).
- Give **at least once a day** whenever an antibiotic course is running.

## Cautions

- Not a treatment on its own — it's supportive care alongside antibiotics, not
  a substitute for addressing the underlying infection.

## History

alongside whatever antibiotic prompted it. Relevant to essentially every

[[Clavamox]]), [[Cass]] ([[Baytril]] + [[Clavamox]]), [[Clarice]] ([[Baytril]]).
