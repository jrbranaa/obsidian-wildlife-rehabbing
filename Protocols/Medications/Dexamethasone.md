## Use

Cranial / CNS inflammation after head trauma — see [[Head Injury]]. Per the
formulary: used for **head trauma, CNS or spinal trauma, shock** (weak,
depressed, pale mucous membranes, cold clammy skin, rapid heartbeat), and
**endotoxemia**. Not used as an anti-fungal.

## Dosing

- **0.5–2 mg/kg**, **IM only** ("hard on intestines" — avoid other routes per
  formulary note). Chart's own reference point is 2 mg/kg IM BID.
- Give as early as possible after a suspected head injury.
- **Use as little as possible** — step the dose/interval down as improvement is
  seen; typical course 2–4 days. After an initial dose, wait 24h to evaluate,
  then begin tapering frequency after a positive response.
- **Should not be mixed with [[Metacam]]** — wait 24h after a single Dex dose,
  or 4-5 days after multiple doses, before giving Metacam.
- Watch for polydipsia (thirst), polyphagia (overeating), polyuria (excess
  urine) as expected side effects.

## Cautions

- Do not combine with NSAIDs ([[Metacam]]) — see timing rule above.
- Immunosuppressive — avoid with active infection unless the vet directs it.
- Never stop a multi-day course abruptly; taper.
- Ensure the patient is warm and hydrated first.
- IM only — do not give SQ or PO per the formulary's GI-tolerance warning.
