> [!warning] Verify before use
> Corticosteroid use in trauma is debated. Confirm concentration, dose, and
> taper plan with your supervising vet before relying on this.

## Use

Cranial / CNS inflammation after head trauma — see [[Head Injury]].

## Dosing

- **0.5–2 mg/kg**, SQ or IM.
- Give as early as possible after a suspected head injury.
- **Use as little as possible** — step the dose/interval down as improvement is
  seen; typical course 2–4 days.

### Dose from weight (at 2 mg/mL)

| Weight | 0.5 mg/kg | 1 mg/kg  | 2 mg/kg  |
| ------ | --------- | -------- | -------- |
| 100 g  | 0.025 mL  | 0.05 mL  | 0.10 mL  |
| 250 g  | 0.06 mL   | 0.13 mL  | 0.25 mL  |
| 500 g  | 0.13 mL   | 0.25 mL  | 0.50 mL  |
| 800 g  | 0.20 mL   | 0.40 mL  | 0.80 mL  |

## Cautions

- Do not combine with NSAIDs ([[Metacam]]).
- Immunosuppressive — avoid with active infection unless the vet directs it.
- Never stop a multi-day course abruptly; taper.
- Ensure the patient is warm and hydrated first.
