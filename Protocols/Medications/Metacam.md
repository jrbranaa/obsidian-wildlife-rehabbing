> [!warning] Verify before use
> Confirm concentration (1.5 mg/mL dog oral vs 0.5 mg/mL cat oral), dose, and
> interval with your supervising vet before relying on this.

## Use

Pain and inflammation — fractures, soft-tissue injury, post-trauma.

## Dosing

- **0.1–0.2 mg/kg**, PO or SQ, once daily.
- Key: the patient **must stay well hydrated** — dehydration + NSAID risks
  kidney/liver damage.
- Give with food when possible. Use the shortest course that controls pain.

### Dose from weight (at 1.5 mg/mL)

| Weight | 0.1 mg/kg | 0.2 mg/kg |
| ------ | --------- | --------- |
| 100 g  | 0.007 mL  | 0.013 mL  |
| 250 g  | 0.017 mL  | 0.033 mL  |
| 500 g  | 0.033 mL  | 0.067 mL  |
| 800 g  | 0.053 mL  | 0.107 mL  |

## Cautions

- Do not combine with corticosteroids ([[Dexamethasone]]).
- Avoid in dehydrated, hypothermic, or shocky patients until stabilized.
- Avoid with known renal/GI compromise.

## History

Log each dose in the patient's **## Medications** table (drug = `Metacam`).
