> From the SWR (May 2026) squirrel dosage chart. Confirm concentration, dose,
> and interval with your supervising vet before relying on this.

## Use

Neuropathic/adjunct pain control — useful alongside [[Metacam]] for
orthopedic injury, nerve involvement, or pain not fully controlled by an
NSAID alone.

## Dosing

- **20 mg/kg**, PO, BID.
- Formulation on this chart: **50 mg/mL**.
- Commercial oral solution often contains **xylitol** — confirm the
  product used is squirrel-safe (avoid compounded formulations with
  xylitol if there's any doubt).

## Cautions

- Sedation possible, especially early in a course — watch for excess grogginess.
- Confirm the specific product's excipients (xylitol concern above).
