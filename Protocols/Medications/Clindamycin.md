## Use

Broad-spectrum oral antibiotic, effective against **many anaerobes**. General
purpose — wound abscesses, osteomyelitis, toxoplasmosis. **Do not use on
rabbits or ruminants.**

## Dosing

- **11 mg/kg** (low dose), PO, BID, for **5-7+ days**.
- Formulation: **25 mg/mL** liquid.
- Food decreases absorption rate.
- May cause stomach upset — pair with probiotic per formulary-wide note.
- Evaluate response at **5-7 days**.
