> [!warning] Verify before use
> From the SWR (May 2026) squirrel dosage chart. Confirm the volume and
> whether it's a daily total or per-dose amount with your supervising vet
> before relying on this.
>
> **Interpretation note:** the chart lists **25 mL/kg** labeled "Daily
> Maintenance" with a frequency of **TID**. Read literally, this is most
> likely the **daily maintenance total**, split across 3 doses — i.e. roughly
> **~8 mL/kg per dose, given 3×/day** — rather than 25 mL/kg given three
> times a day (which would be a much larger volume). Confirm this reading
> before dosing.

## Use

General fluid support / rehydration — dehydration from diarrhea, reduced
intake, heat stress, or as supportive care alongside antibiotics that
require good hydration ([[TMS]], enrofloxacin/Baytril).

## Dosing

- **25 mL/kg/day** (daily maintenance), divided **TID** — see interpretation
  note above.
- Route: **SQ** for a real deficit / when oral intake is unreliable; **PO**
  (small, frequent amounts) for mild deficits in an animal still eating/drinking.
- Warm to body temperature before giving.

### Daily total volume from weight (25 mL/kg/day, per SWR chart)

| Weight | Daily total | Per dose (÷3, TID) |
| ------ | ----------- | -------------------- |
| 50 g   | 1.25 mL | ~0.4 mL |
| 100 g  | 2.5 mL  | ~0.8 mL |
| 200 g  | 5.0 mL  | ~1.7 mL |
| 300 g  | 7.5 mL  | ~2.5 mL |
| 500 g  | 12.5 mL | ~4.2 mL |
| 1000 g | 25.0 mL | ~8.3 mL |

## Cautions

- This is a **maintenance** rate for a mildly-to-moderately dehydrated but
  stable patient — a patient in real crisis (e.g. hypothermic, shocky) needs
  vet-directed deficit + maintenance fluid math, not just this table.
- Warm the fluid — cold SQ fluids are uncomfortable and can worsen hypothermia.
- Reassess frequently (skin tent, gum moisture) rather than dosing on autopilot.
