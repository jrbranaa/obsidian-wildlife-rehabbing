How to mix, supplement, store, and deliver formula. See [[Feeding Schedule]]
for how much/how often by age, and [[Intake Procedure]] for the
rehydration-before-formula sequence.

## Formula type

**Fox Valley Day 1 Squirrel Formula** — closest match to mother squirrel's
milk of the options tested.

- **32/40** — squirrels **under 4 weeks**.
- **20/50** — squirrels **4 weeks and older**.
- Base ratio: **2 parts bottled water (not distilled) : 1 part Fox Valley
  powder.** Distilled water contains no minerals — don't use it.
- Switching 32/40 → 20/50: **beginning at 4-5 weeks**, blend gradually — mix
  20/50 diluted at 3:1 for a couple feedings up to 24h, then move gradually to
  full-strength 2:1. (Matches the operator's own gradual-blend approach
  already in use for [[Abby]].)

## Supplements

- **Lactobacillus acidophilus (LA 200) + L-Glutamine**: 1/16 tsp each per 1/4
  cup of the 2:1 formula mix. Scale up proportionally for larger batches
  (e.g. 8T water + 4T powder + 1/8 tsp each). Add from the very beginning for
  intestinal benefit/immune support.
- **Bioplasma** — "cell salts," improves health of a sick/injured squirrel;
  can be given separately at feeding time.
- **DMG** — immune system boost, used sometimes.

## Mixing & storage

- Small jar with a tight lid for mixing; shake or whip thoroughly. If mixing
  in advance, refrigerate several hours to let air bubbles dissipate — or
  stir gently and pour through a sieve to press out clumps (usable right
  away, no bubble wait needed).
- **Ensure no air bubbles before feeding** — bubbles can upset the stomach.
- **Refrigerate mixed formula for 24 hours only** — make a new batch daily.
- **Warm only the amount needed for one feeding**, in the syringe, in very
  hot water. **Never microwave the formula itself** — destroys nutritional
  value (microwaving the water bath is fine).
- Target feeding temperature: **~70-72°F**, test on the inside of your wrist.
  Feed immediately while warm; reheat if it cools before you're done.
- **Discard any leftover formula in the syringe after feeding — do not
  reuse.**
- Store powdered Fox Valley and all supplements **in the freezer** — long
  shelf life there. Watch expiration dates.
- For babies up to 3-4 weeks (especially if sick or feeding poorly), mix only
  enough for 1-2 feedings at a time — fresher formula, less bacterial risk.

## Equipment & hygiene

- **Luer-Lok, centered-tip, O-ring feeding syringe** (1, 3, 5, or 10cc
  depending on age) — **never a PetNurser-style bottle**, which delivers too
  much air and raises aspiration/bloat risk (see [[Aspiration]], [[Bloating]]).
- Nipple: Catac/Zoologic/Miracle for pinkies; 4-Paws from ~2 weeks on.
- If the nipple has no hole, snip a **very small** hole (heat a size-18 needle
  and work it in/out to enlarge slightly if needed) — usable birth to 8 weeks;
  older babies may need a slightly wider opening, or a razor-slit tip
  ("X" slit for older babies).
- After feeding: wash equipment thoroughly with hot water and a small bottle
  brush. **Sterilize every few days** — boil 2 minutes, or soak in a
  disinfectant (e.g. Nolvasan) and rinse thoroughly with hot water.
