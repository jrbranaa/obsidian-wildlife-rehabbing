The most common problem. Causes: sudden diet change, too-rich formula,

## Recognizing progression

Watch for stool becoming sticky/elongated/softer-than-normal (pudding
consistency = "soft stool") or watery/free-flowing ("diarrhea"), and/or color
shifting brighter yellow, whitish, or greenish.

## Early/soft-stool stage — dietary steps first

- Lengthen the interval between feedings (food may be moving too fast for
  proper water reabsorption).
- Consider dropping a feeding, feeding higher volume at the remaining ones.
- If feeding more than 5% body weight, delay the next feeding and cap
  subsequent feeds at 5%.
- Dilute the formula (2:1 → try 3:1 for several feedings; if no improvement,
  4:1; reverse as it improves).
- If weaning, pull back any new solid foods to see if that's the driver.
- Confirm feeding equipment is clean; minimize stress generally.

## Full diarrhea — sudden onset

- Skip the next feeding entirely.
- Next feeding: hydration only at 5% (Ringer's/Electrostat) for 1-2 feedings
  to let the GI tract empty.
- Resume formula at 5%, with a longer interval between feedings.

## Antibiotic-associated diarrhea

- **Give [[BeneBac]] (or Probiacin)** whenever an antibiotic is running —
  roughly matching the antibiotic's own volume (e.g. 0.1cc Clavamox → 0.1cc
  BeneBac). Out-of-balance flora from antibiotic use can turn stool orange,
  rust-colored, or green.

## Internal parasites

Most common: **Coccidia** (by far the most prevalent), then roundworms,
hookworms, Giardia. Signs: diarrhea, mucus/blood in stool, appetite loss,
slow growth, weight loss (mild to severe emaciation), general debilitation,
sometimes vomiting.
- **Coccidia**: often too-soft/voluminous/smelly stool, sometimes bloody.
- **Giardia**: often greenish color.

**Get a fecal sample tested if at all possible** — then treat specifically:
- Confirmed **Coccidia** or **Giardia** → **[[Flagyl]]** for Giardia,
  **[[Baycox]] or [[Albon]]** for Coccidia.
- Confirmed **worms** → **[[Panacur]]** (worms are uncommon — rule out other
  causes first).
