## Ear mites

Suspect if scratching ears/shaking head frequently, or: black residue in
ears, redness/inflammation, blood from scratching, chewed-looking ear edges.

- Apply mineral oil inside the ear with a Q-tip, let sit several minutes.
- Gently remove black residue; put the used Q-tip in a sealed plastic bag
  with more mineral oil to kill the mites.

## Fleas

- Sprinkle a small amount of **kitten flea powder** on a piece of fleece,
  placed in the basket/cage **near, not on**, the squirrel.
- **[[Capstar]]** is an ingestible option but rarely used in practice.
