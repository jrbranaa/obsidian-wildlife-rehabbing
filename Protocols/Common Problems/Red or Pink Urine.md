source: Sierra Wildlife Rescue Squirrel Manual (2025), by Nan Powers and Linda Mueller

Rodents process a blood component (uroporphyrin) that can tint urine
reddish — **Fox squirrels especially**. Reddish urine that's clear (not
cloudy) may just be normal. True bloody urine can signal internal injury or
UTI — weigh the squirrel's history (recent trauma, symptoms) when deciding
whether to treat for injury/UTI or just monitor.
