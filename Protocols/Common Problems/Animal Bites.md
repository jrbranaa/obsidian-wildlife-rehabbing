## Wound care

- Spread vaseline or antibiotic gel on the hair around the wound, then trim
  the hair away — the gel lets clipped hair be wiped away instead of falling
  into the wound.
- Clean the injury with an antibiotic solution (Nolvasan or Vetericin),
  getting solution **deep into the wound**. Repeat several times a day until
  healing/infection-free.
- Topical antibiotic cream on the affected area.

## Systemic treatment

- **Begin antibiotics immediately** — **Baytril and/or Clavamox** are the
  first choices. If infection doesn't improve, a combination may be needed.

This matches the plan already started for Clarice (empiric Baytril for

that choice, and flags Clavamox as the natural second agent to add if Baytril
alone isn't enough (same escalation pattern already used for [[Abby]] and

## Cat bites specifically

See [[Clavamox]] — it's specifically called out elsewhere in this formulary
as effective against cat-bite *Pasteurella*, which is the main organism of
concern for a cat-inflicted wound even if it looks minor on the surface.
