Less common than diarrhea but can be severe/life-threatening. Caused by
overfeeding, swallowing too much air, or more serious problems (parasites).
Relevant to [[OB]]'s bloat/lethargy episode during his GI case.

## Recognizing it

Stomach feels **taut**, not just full. Early sign: seems hungry but becomes
satiated very quickly.

## Care

- Stimulate genitals, then place stomach-down on a heating pad (Low) or a
  warm wet washcloth; gently massage lower back/stomach.
- Defecating or passing gas may relieve it.
- A drop or two of **infant Simethicone** can help.
- Next feeding: reduce volume, make sure he isn't swallowing air.
