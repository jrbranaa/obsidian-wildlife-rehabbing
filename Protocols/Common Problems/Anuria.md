Inability to urinate — generally from dehydration. Normal for a new squirrel
not to urinate 12-24h after receipt; beyond that, may need help. Can also
occur from a cagemate's [[Genital Sucking]] leaving little/no urine at
stimulation. Darker urine color = held in the bladder too long, needs more
frequent stimulation.

## Care

- Additional hydration: Ringer's/Electrostat, 5% body weight every 2h for 2
  cycles.
- Gently massage the lower abdomen, moving downward toward the urethral
  opening.
- Homeopathic: Plumbum.
