---
source: Sierra Wildlife Rescue Squirrel Manual (2025), by Nan Powers and Linda Mueller
---

Squirrels molt normally, but **patchy** fur loss with redness or actual sores
may be mange mites, ringworm/other fungus, or bacterial skin infection. May
need vet input.

- **[[Ivermectin]]** for mange/mites — one drop behind the ear, or one drop
  fed on/injected into a walnut; repeat weekly.
