Bordetella has "recently appeared" in tree squirrels per this manual (same
bacterium as canine kennel cough). Was one of the pathogens considered in
[[Cass]]'s working case model (ultimately assessed as less likely than
opportunistic infection from her own aspiration problem — see her file).

## Signs

Lethargy, respiratory symptoms, profuse/frequent urination, sudden weight
loss — all, some, or none may be present at onset.

## Treatment

**If caught early: treatable with [[TMS]] and supportive care.** If not
caught early, **can lead to sudden death** — this is the urgency case for
treating a suspicious respiratory presentation promptly rather than waiting
to see if it resolves on its own.
