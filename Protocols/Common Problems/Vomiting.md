A squirrel that comes in thin and vomits after every feeding may have
esophageal parasites, injury, poisoning, or a GI disorder. **See a wildlife
vet immediately** — this is one of the few symptoms in this manual flagged as
not a home-management situation. Poisoning is also a possible cause.
