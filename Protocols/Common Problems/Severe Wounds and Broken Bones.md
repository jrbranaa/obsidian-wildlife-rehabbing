Treatability depends on severity, location, and age. Head/facial injuries can
usually be managed by a rehabber; broken extremities often need a vet to set.
Head injuries (broken nasal cartilage, jaw injuries, other head trauma) are
common — babies often land on their heads/noses when they fall. See
[[Head Injury]] for the operator's own working protocol.

## Head trauma / pain

- **[[Metacam]]** — swelling/pain reduction in less serious cases.
- **[[Dexamethasone]]** — more severe head injuries, potential nerve/spinal
  damage. (Do not combine — see timing rule in Dexamethasone's file.)
- Antibiotics for any open wound (oral or topical, depending on
  type/severity).
- **Veterinary care needed** for broken bones or deep wounds needing sutures;
  X-rays if a broken back is suspected.
