Not uncommon among caged littermates/cagemates — can cause abrasions, deeper
wounds, or infection. Usually the penis, but any body part (including
self-sucking) is possible. Possible causes: teething, stress, inadequate
fluids, urinary infection, insufficient post-feed stimulation, or simply an
unsatisfied sucking urge. More frequent feedings with smaller amounts may
help but may not fully stop it.

## Care

- Treat an injured penis topically (Neosporin/triple-antibiotic/other
  ointment).
- Bitter Apple or "Yuk" ointment around the genital region of the *injured*
  squirrel (not on abrasions) to discourage the sucking nest-mate.
- Sucking often causes penile swelling or a urethral-opening scab, blocking
  urine flow — soak with warm water to remove the scab, repeat until
  swelling resolves and urine flows freely (see also [[Anuria]]).
- Treat any inflammation suggestive of infection with an oral antibiotic.
- If it persists: separate the squirrels (perpetrator to a nearby cage).
  Reuniting can be tried after a few days — watch closely for recurrence.
