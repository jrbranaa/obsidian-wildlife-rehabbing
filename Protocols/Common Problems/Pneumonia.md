Caused by bacterial/viral infection, or fluid in the lungs from aspiration
(see [[Aspiration]]) or cold/damp exposure (especially in infants). Directly
relevant to [[Cass]]'s case.

## Signs

Lethargy; difficult/labored/rapid breathing; wheezing, crackling, or clicking
sounds; fever; nasal discharge; may move to the top of the bedding.

## Treatment

- **[[Nuflor]] along with [[Baytril]]** — the manual's stated best combo
  ("best treatment is Nuflor" for pneumonia, "Nuflor along with Baytril" is
  the fuller regimen listed).
- Keep warm, well hydrated, and quiet.
- Clean, smoke/dust-free environment.
- Phosphorus (homeopathic).

> [!info] Not currently the regimen in use
> Cass's active pneumonia/aspiration treatment has been Clavamox + Baytril +
> nebulized gentamicin (chosen for on-hand-drug availability during her acute
> crisis) and she is responding well. No reason to switch a working plan, but
> if a new pneumonia case comes up, Nuflor + Baytril is the formulary's actual
> first-line combo — see [[Nuflor]].
