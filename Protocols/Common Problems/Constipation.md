Normal not to defecate for 12-24h after intake; beyond that may need help.
Signs: infrequent/absent defecation, or very small/hard/dark stool that's
slow/difficult to pass. Causes: dehydration, injury, intestinal blockage,
under-stimulation, or other.

## Care options

- Additional hydration: Ringer's/Electrostat, 5% body weight every 2h for 2
  cycles.
- Homeopathic: Plumbum (pinkies only, per this chart).
- Karo syrup: 2 drops per 10cc formula, or 0.2-0.3cc undiluted orally, once.
- Pumpkin puree: 0.4cc mixed with a little water; usually works after one
  dose, may repeat after 4h. (Freeze leftover puree in an ice cube tray.)
- Pineapple juice: 0.3cc; usually works after one dose, may repeat after 4h.
- Papaya tablet: crush 1, dissolve in 3cc water, give 1cc; usually works
  after one dose, may repeat after 4h.
- Gently massage the lower GI tract, across the lower abdomen toward the
  anus.
