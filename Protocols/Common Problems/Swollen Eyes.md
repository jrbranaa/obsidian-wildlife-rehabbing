Occasionally an infant's eyes swell **before they open**, from infection —
more severe-looking than the normal "bulgy" pre-open look at birth-10 days.
**Needs to be seen/treated by a wildlife vet**, who may prescribe an
antibiotic eye ointment. Older babies can also develop eye infections, with
specialized ointments/drops available.

Relevant background for [[Clarice]] (closed, possibly-injured left eye at
intake) — though her presentation (dried, not-fresh discharge, associated
with a cat-cornering incident) reads more like injury/discharge than the
pre-open infectious swelling this section describes; still worth knowing this
is a recognized, vet-treatable presentation if her eye doesn't resolve with
gentle cleaning.
