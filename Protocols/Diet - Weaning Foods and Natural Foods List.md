Reference food lists for weaning/weaned squirrels. See [[Development Stages]]
for when to start introducing solids, and [[Metabolic Bone Disease]] for why
calcium-rich choices matter as formula intake drops.

## Foods for weaning or weaned squirrels

Introduce gradually, one or two new items at a time; research/consult before
trying anything not on this list, and match to the actual species being
rehabbed.

**Nuts & seeds** (also leaves/branches): walnuts, almonds, chestnuts, pecans,
hazelnuts (filberts), pine cones/pine nuts, pumpkin seeds, squash seeds,
grains (all kinds).

**Vegetables**: alfalfa/clover sprouts, artichoke leaves (remove to feed*),
broccoli, brussels sprouts, carrots, collard greens, corn on the cob (pieces),
jicama (ground squirrels especially), kale, lettuce (not iceberg — "Spring
Mix" varieties are good), mushrooms (white/brown), mustard greens, spinach,
sweet potato/yams, squash (all kinds).

**Fruits**: apples, apricots, avocado (**one piece/day max**, no skin/leaves/
pit/stem), blackberries, blueberries, cherries, cranberries*, strawberries*,
grapes, loquats, melons (watermelon/cantaloupe), oranges*, tangerines/
mandarins*, peaches, pears, plums, prickly pear, pyracantha berries,
raspberries, watermelon.

**Misc**: Zoopreem primate/animal diet, homemade squirrel chow, rose petals
and hips, buds/flowers (camellia, rose, manzanita etc. — unfertilized/
unsprayed only, check edible-flower safety first), unsweetened Cheerios.

**\*Hold until at least 8 weeks old**: artichoke leaves, cranberries,
strawberries, oranges, tangerines/mandarins.

**DON'Ts**: no highly-acidic fruit before 8 weeks (oranges etc.), **never
salted nuts**, **never peanuts or sunflower seeds** for a baby/juvenile — a
diet restricted to those lacks essential amino acids for calcium absorption
and can cause Metabolic Bone Disease.

## Natural foods list by species

**Gray & Fox Squirrel**: acorns, conifer underbark/leaves/cones/nuts/buds,
oak (acorns/underbark/leaves), maple underbark, fungi/lichen/moss, English &
black walnuts, hazelnuts, spruce, cedar, ash, dogwood flowers, manzanita
(flowers & berries), raspberry, blueberry, holly berry, hackberry, elderberry,
pyracantha, wild grapes, miner's lettuce, other fruits/berries.

**Douglas Squirrel** (Chickaree/Pine Squirrel): pine, fir, Douglas fir, cedar,
hazelnut, ash (underbark/leaves/buds/cones/nuts), fungi/lichen/moss, fruits/
berries, grains, manzanita (flowers & berries), miner's lettuce, seeds/nuts.

**Northern Flying Squirrel**: acorns, fungi/lichen/moss (heavily), grains,
fruits/berries, manzanita (flowers & berries), miner's lettuce, conifer
underbark/leaves/cones/nuts/buds, seeds/nuts, flying insects.

**California Ground Squirrel / Chipmunk**: oak, acorns, lupine (flower &
seed), wild oats (veg & seed), seeds/nuts, manzanita (flowers & berries),
buckeye, fungi/lichen/moss, miner's lettuce.
