## Notes

### 2024.09.12

Mace passed on in my hand this evening.  He'd taken a steep down turn this morning and really wouldn't eat much and kept laying out in the open. He felt cool to the touch.  It appeared that his bulging eye had popped as it was dried out looking and not very large.  He had what appeared to be dried mucus/puss around his ears (not sure where that came from, maybe his eye), and he had a clicking when breathing.  It seemed that he may have some infection going on so I decided to dose him with baytril.  Unfortunately I'd forgotten that there are two types - full strength and 1:10 dilution. We have the full strength one.  As a result I over dosed him and am concerned that this may have been too much.  I don't know if it would have resulted in death, but I feel really bad about any part I played in his death or suffering.  THAT WONT HAPPEN AGAIN!

Anyways, I was saddened to see her pass. We buried her under our hydrangeas pot behind the hammock. RIP Mace and I comforted in knowing that you are in heaven living your best life there.  

### 2024.09.10

Took mace to the vet for her eye. Was given some additional meds for her eyes, to keep them lubricated. Pressure test on right eye seemed ok, but it does have an ulcer suggesting her blinking abnormally.  Her left eye is probably going ro have to be removed when she's older.  Apt in 2 months to check on progress. 

It appears that she may have eye issues.  Linda has been treating as needed with 1 drop each eye twice a day.
